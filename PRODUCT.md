# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Ludora initially serves groups of friends who already play board games together
and want to keep playing when they are apart.

## Product Purpose

Ludora brings a group's board game night online. The goal of the first playable
release is for the group to go from meeting up to starting a match in Ludora.

## Positioning

The room comes before the game choice. One friend creates a room, the group
gathers there, and they choose what to play from inside the room.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Today, players can register and verify their email, sign in with a password or
  Google, manage their account, and update their profile with a preset avatar
  and biography.
- Games, ranking, and account activity currently display empty states. Rooms
  and playable games are goals for the first playable release.
- Future matches must keep their state on the server so a player can reconnect
  after an interruption.
- The web interface supports English and Spanish and has light and dark themes.
- The first supported game, how friends join a room, and who controls game
  selection are open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

Use the Ludora name and the existing logo across both themes. The source artwork
is in `apps/web/src/components/branding/ludora-logo.tsx`.

## Evidence on Hand

The current user flows and their limitations are documented in
`apps/web/README.md`. Public copy should reflect the features available in the
running app.
